Abstract

摘要

Sequential prediction problems such as imitation learning, where future observations depend on previous predictions (actions), violate the common i.i.d. assumptions made in statistical learning. This leads to poor performance in theory and often in practice. Some recent approaches (Daumé III et al., 2009; Ross and Bagnell, 2010) provide stronger guarantees in this setting, but remain somewhat unsatisfactory as they train either non-stationary or stochastic policies and require a large number of iterations. In this paper, we propose a new iterative algorithm, which trains a stationary deterministic policy, that can be seen as a no regret algorithm in an online learning setting. We show that any such no regret algorithm, combined with additional reduction assumptions, must find a policy with good performance under the distribution of observations it induces in such sequential settings. We demonstrate that this new approach outperforms previous approaches on two challenging imitation learning problems and a benchmark sequence labeling problem.

We propose a new meta-algorithm for imitation learning which learns a stationary deterministic policy guaranteed to perform well under its induced distribution of states (number of mistakes/costs that grows linearly in \( T \) and classification cost \( \epsilon \) ). We take a reduction-based approach (Beygelzimer et al., 2005) that enables reusing existing supervised learning algorithms. Our approach is simple to implement, has no free parameters except the supervised learning algorithm sub-routine, and requires a number of iterations that scales nearly linearly with the effective horizon of the problem. It naturally handles continuous as well as discrete predictions. Our approach is closely related to no regret online learning algorithms (Cesa-Bianchi et al., 2004; Hazan et al., 2006; Kakade and Shalev-Shwartz, 2008) (in particular Follow-The-Leader ) but better leverages the expert in our setting. Additionally, we show that any no-regret learner can be used in a particular fashion to learn a policy that achieves similar guarantees.

我们提出了一种新的模仿学习元算法，该算法学习一个平稳确定性策略，保证在其诱导的状态分布下表现良好（错误数/代价关于 \( T \) 和分类代价 \( \epsilon \) 呈线性增长）。我们采用基于归约的方法（Beygelzimer et al., 2005），能够复用现有的监督学习算法。我们的方法易于实现，除监督学习算法子程序外没有自由参数，并且所需的迭代次数与问题的有效时界呈近线性缩放。它能够自然地处理连续预测和离散预测。我们的方法与无遗憾在线学习算法（Cesa-Bianchi et al., 2004; Hazan et al., 2006; Kakade and Shalev-Shwartz, 2008）（特别是 Follow-The-Leader）密切相关，但在我们的设定下能更好地利用专家。此外，我们证明了任何无遗憾学习器都可以通过特定方式用于学习能够达到类似保证的策略。

2 PRELIMINARIES

2 预备知识

We begin by introducing notation relevant to our setting. We denote by \( \Pi \) the class of policies the learner is considering and \( T \) the task horizon. For any policy \( \pi \), we let \( d^t_\pi \) denote the distribution of states at time \( t \) if the learner executed policy \( \pi \) from time step \( 1 \) to \( t - 1 \). Furthermore, we denote \( d_\pi = \frac{1}{T} \sum_{t=1}^T d^t_\pi \) the average distribution of states if we follow policy \( \pi \) for \( T \) steps. Given a state \( s \), we denote \( C(s,a) \) the expected immediate cost of performing action \( a \) in state \( s \) for the task we are considering and denote \( C_\pi(s) = \mathbb{E}_{a \sim \pi(s)}[C(s,a)] \) the expected immediate cost of \( \pi \) in \( s \). We assume \( C \) is bounded in \( [0, 1] \). The total cost of executing policy \( \pi \) for \( T \)-steps ( i.e. , the cost-to-go) is denoted \( J(\pi) = \sum_{t=1}^T \mathbb{E}_{s \sim d^t_\pi}[C_\pi(s)] = T \mathbb{E}_{s \sim d_\pi}[C_\pi(s)] \).

我们首先介绍与我们设定相关的符号。我们用 \( \Pi \) 表示学习者所考虑的策略类，用 \( T \) 表示任务时界。对于任意策略 \( \pi \)，我们用 \( d^t_\pi \) 表示如果学习者从时间步 \( 1 \) 到 \( t - 1 \) 执行策略 \( \pi \) 时在时间 \( t \) 的状态分布。此外，我们用 \( d_\pi = \frac{1}{T} \sum_{t=1}^T d^t_\pi \) 表示如果遵循策略 \( \pi \) 执行 \( T \) 步时的平均状态分布。给定状态 \( s \)，我们用 \( C(s,a) \) 表示在我们所考虑的任务中在状态 \( s \) 下执行动作 \( a \) 的期望即时代价，并用 \( C_\pi(s) = \mathbb{E}_{a \sim \pi(s)}[C(s,a)] \) 表示 \( \pi \) 在 \( s \) 下的期望即时代价。我们假设 \( C \) 有界于 \( [0, 1] \)。执行策略 \( \pi \) 经历 \( T \)-steps 的总代价（即 cost-to-go）记为 \( J(\pi) = \sum_{t=1}^T \mathbb{E}_{s \sim d^t_\pi}[C_\pi(s)] = T \mathbb{E}_{s \sim d_\pi}[C_\pi(s)] \)。

In imitation learning, we may not necessarily know or observe true costs \( C(s,a) \) for the particular task. Instead, we observe expert demonstrations and seek to bound \( J(\pi) \) for any cost function \( C \) based on how well \( \pi \) mimics the expert’s policy \( \pi^* \). Denote \( \ell \) the observed surrogate loss function we minimize instead of \( C \). For instance \( \ell(s,\pi) \) may be the expected 0-1 loss of \( \pi \) with respect to \( \pi^* \) in state \( s \), or a squared/hinge loss of \( \pi \) with respect to \( \pi^* \) in \( s \). Importantly, in many instances, \( C \) and \( \ell \) may be the same function– for instance, if we are interested in optimizing the learner’s ability to predict the actions chosen by an expert.

在模仿学习中，对于特定任务，我们不一定知晓或能够观测到真实代价 \( C(s,a) \)。相反，我们观测专家示教，并力求基于 \( \pi \) 模仿专家策略 \( \pi^* \) 的优劣程度，对任意代价函数 \( C \) 的 \( J(\pi) \) 给出界。记 \( \ell \) 为我们用以替代 \( C \) 进行最小化的观测代理损失函数。例如，\( \ell(s,\pi) \) 可以是状态 \( s \) 下 \( \pi \) 相对于 \( \pi^* \) 的期望 0-1 损失，或者是状态 \( s \) 下 \( \pi \) 相对于 \( \pi^* \) 的平方/合页损失。重要的是，在许多情况下，\( C \) 与 \( \ell \) 可以是同一个函数——例如，如果我们关注的是优化学习者预测专家所选动作的能力。

我们的目标是找到一个策略 \( \hat{\pi} \)，以最小化在其诱导的状态分布下的观测代理损失，即：

As system dynamics are assumed both unknown and complex, we cannot compute \(d_\pi\) and can only sample it by executing \(\pi\) in the system. Hence this is a non-i.i.d. supervised learning problem due to the dependence of the input distribution on the policy \(\pi\) itself. The interaction between policy and the resulting distribution makes optimization difficult as it results in a non-convex objective even if the loss \(\ell(s,\cdot)\) is convex in \(\pi\) for all states \(s\). We now briefly review previous approaches and their guarantees.

由于系统动力学被假定为既未知又复杂，我们无法直接计算 \(d_\pi\)，只能通过在系统中执行 \(\pi\) 对其进行采样。因此，由于输入分布对策略 \(\pi\) 自身存在依赖性，这是一个非 i.i.d. 的监督学习问题。策略与所产生分布之间的相互作用使得优化变得困难，因为即使损失 \(\ell(s,\cdot)\) 对所有状态 \(s\) 关于 \(\pi\) 都是凸的，它也会导致非凸的目标函数。下面我们简要回顾以往的方法及其理论保证。

2.2 Forward Training

2.2 前向训练

The forward training algorithm introduced by Ross and Bagnell (2010) trains a non-stationary policy (one policy \(\pi_t\) for each time step \(t\)) iteratively over \(T\) iterations, where at iteration \(t\), \(\pi_t\) is trained to mimic \(\pi^*\) on the distribution of states at time \(t\) induced by the previously trained policies \(\pi_1, \pi_2, \dots, \pi_{t-1}\). By doing so, \(\pi_t\) is trained on the actual distribution of states it will encounter during execution of the learned policy. Hence the forward algorithm guarantees that the expected loss under the distribution of states induced by the learned policy matches the average loss during training, and hence improves performance.

Ross and Bagnell (2010) 提出的前向训练算法在 \(T\) 次迭代中迭代训练一个非平稳策略（每个时间步 \(t\) 对应一个策略 \(\pi_t\)）。在迭代 \(t\) 中，\(\pi_t\) 被训练为在先前训练的策略 \(\pi_1, \pi_2, \dots, \pi_{t-1}\) 所诱导的时间 \(t\) 的状态分布上模仿 \(\pi^*\)。通过这种方式，\(\pi_t\) 是在其执行所学策略期间实际会遇到的状态分布上进行训练的。因此，前向算法保证了在所学策略诱导的状态分布下的期望损失与训练期间的平均损失相匹配，从而提升了性能。

2.3 Stochastic Mixing Iterative Learning

2.3 随机混合迭代学习

SMILe, proposed by Ross and Bagnell (2010), alleviates this problem and can be applied in practice when \(T\) is large or undefined by adopting an approach similar to SEARN (Daumé III et al., 2009) where a stochastic stationary policy is trained over several iterations. Initially SMILe starts with a policy \(\pi_0\) which always queries and executes the expert’s action choice. At iteration \(n\), a policy \(\hat{\pi}_n\) is trained to mimic the expert under the distribution of trajectories \(\pi_{n-1}\) induces and then updates \(\pi_n = \pi_{n-1} + \alpha(1-\alpha)^{n-1}(\hat{\pi}_n - \pi_0)\). This update is interpreted as adding probability \(\alpha(1-\alpha)^{n-1}\) to executing policy \(\hat{\pi}_n\) at any step and removing probability \(\alpha(1-\alpha)^{n-1}\) of executing the queried expert’s action. At iteration \(n\), \(\pi_n\) is a mixture of \(n\) policies and the probability of using the queried expert’s action is \((1-\alpha)^n\). We can stop the algorithm at any iteration \(N\) by returning the re-normalized policy \(\tilde{\pi}_N = \frac{\pi_N - (1-\alpha)^N \pi_0}{1-(1-\alpha)^N}\) which doesn’t query the expert anymore. Ross and Bagnell (2010) showed that choosing \(\alpha\) in \(O(\frac{1}{T^2})\) and \(N\) in \(O(T^2 \log T)\) guaranteesnear-linear regret in \(T\) and \(\epsilon\) for some class of problems.

Ross and Bagnell (2010) 提出的 SMILe 缓解了这一问题；通过采用类似于 SEARN（Daumé III et al., 2009）的方法，在多个迭代中训练一个随机平稳策略，SMILe 能够在 \(T\) 很大或未定义时应用于实践。最初，SMILe 从一个始终查询并执行专家所选动作的策略 \(\pi_0\) 开始。在迭代 \(n\)，训练一个策略 \(\hat{\pi}_n\)，用于在 \(\pi_{n-1}\) 所诱导的轨迹分布下模仿专家，随后更新 \(\pi_n = \pi_{n-1} + \alpha(1-\alpha)^{n-1}(\hat{\pi}_n - \pi_0)\)。这一更新被解释为：在任意步骤执行策略 \(\hat{\pi}_n\) 的概率增加 \(\alpha(1-\alpha)^{n-1}\)，而执行被查询专家的动作的概率减少 \(\alpha(1-\alpha)^{n-1}\)。在迭代 \(n\)，\(\pi_n\) 是 \(n\) 个策略的混合，使用被查询专家动作的概率为 \((1-\alpha)^n\)。我们可以在任意迭代 \(N\) 停止算法，返回不再查询专家的重新归一化策略\(\tilde{\pi}_N = \frac{\pi_N - (1-\alpha)^N \pi_0}{1-(1-\alpha)^N}\)。Ross and Bagnell (2010) 证明，选择 \(\alpha\) 属于\(O(\frac{1}{T^2})\)且 \(N\) 属于\(O(T^2 \log T)\)能够保证对于某些类别的问题在 \(T\) 和 \(\epsilon\) 上实现近线性遗憾。

4 理论分析

The theoretical analysis of DAGGER only relies on the noregret property of the underlying Follow-The-Leader algorithm on strongly convex losses (Kakade and Tewari, 2009) which picks the sequence of policies \(\hat{\pi}_{1:N}\). Hence the presented results also hold for any other no regret online learning algorithm we would apply to our imitation learning setting. In particular, we can consider the results here a reduction of imitation learning to no-regret online learning where we treat mini-batches of trajectories under a single policy as a single online-learning example. We first briefly review concepts of online learning and no regret that will be used for this analysis.

DAGGER 的理论分析仅依赖于底层“跟随领导者”（Follow-The-Leader）算法在强凸损失上的无悔（no-regret）性质（Kakade and Tewari, 2009），该算法选取了策略序列 \(\hat{\pi}_{1:N}\)。因此，所给出的结果对于我们应用到动作模仿设定中的任何其他无悔在线学习算法同样成立。特别是，我们可以将本文的结果视为将动作模仿归约为无悔在线学习，其中我们将单一策略下的轨迹小批量（mini-batch）视为单个在线学习样本。我们首先简要回顾将用于本分析的在线学习与无悔概念。

6 FUTURE WORK

6 未来工作

We show that by batching over iterations of interaction with a system, no-regret methods, including the presented DAGGER approach can provide a learning reduction with strong performance guarantees in both imitation learning and structured prediction. In future work, we will consider more sophisticated strategies than simple greedy forward decoding for structured prediction, as well as using base classifiers that rely on Inverse Optimal Control (Abbeel and Ng, 2004; Ratliff et al., 2006) techniques to learn a cost function for a planner to aid prediction in imitation learning. Further we believe techniques similar to those presented, by leveraging a cost-to-go estimate, may provide an understanding of the success of online methods for reinforcement learning and suggest a similar data-aggregation method that can guarantee performance in such settings.

我们证明，通过对与系统交互的各次迭代进行批处理，包括本文提出的 DAGGER 方法在内的无悔（no-regret）方法能够在模仿学习和结构化预测中提供具有强大性能保证的学习归约。在未来的工作中，我们将考虑在结构化预测中采用比简单贪心前向解码更复杂的策略，并使用基于逆最优控制（Inverse Optimal Control, Abbeel and Ng, 2004; Ratliff et al., 2006）技术的基分类器来为规划器学习代价函数，以辅助模仿学习中的预测。此外，我们认为类似于本文提出的技术，通过利用 cost-to-go 估计，可能有助于理解在线强化学习方法的成功机理，并启发类似的数据聚合（data-aggregation）方法以在此类场景下提供性能保证。
