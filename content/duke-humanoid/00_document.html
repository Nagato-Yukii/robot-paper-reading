The Duke Humanoid: Design and Control For Energy-Efficient Bipedal Locomotion Using Passive Dynamics

Duke Humanoid：基于被动动力学的高能效双足运动设计与控制

Boxi Xia, Bokuan Li, Jacob Lee, Michael Scutari, Boyuan Chen

Boxi Xia, Bokuan Li, Jacob Lee, Michael Scutari, Boyuan Chen

Abstract — We present the Duke Humanoid, an open-source 10-degrees-of-freedom humanoid, as an extensible platform for locomotion research. The design mimics human physiology, with symmetrical body alignment in the frontal plane to maintain static balance with straight knees. We develop a reinforcement learning policy that can be deployed zero-shot on the hardware for velocity-tracking walking tasks. Additionally, to enhance energy efficiency in locomotion, we propose an end-to-end reinforcement learning algorithm that encourages the robot to leverage passive dynamics. Our experimental results show that our passive policy reduces the cost of transport by up to 50% in simulation and 31% in real-world tests. Our website is http: //generalroboticslab.com/DukeHumanoidv1/.

摘要 — 我们推出了 Duke Humanoid，这是一款开源的 10 自由度人形机器人，作为用于运动研究的可扩展平台。该设计模仿人体生理结构，在额状面上具有对称的身体对齐结构，从而能够以直膝姿态保持静态平衡。我们开发了一种强化学习策略，可零样本（zero-shot）部署于硬件上以完成速度跟踪行走任务。此外，为了提高运动中的能量效率，我们提出了一种端到端强化学习算法，鼓励机器人利用被动动力学。实验结果表明，我们的被动策略在仿真中将运动能耗成本（cost of transport）降低了多达 50%，在真实世界测试中降低了 31%。我们的网站为 http://generalroboticslab.com/DukeHumanoidv1/。

I. INTRODUCTION

I. 引言

Recent advances in robotics have enabled humanoids to perform impressive dynamic motions, such as walking, high-speed running, and backflipping [1–4]. However, many commercially available humanoids are closed systems with limited customizability and access to low-level sensor and actuator interfaces. Some recent efforts open-source the software [5, 6], while others offer open-source hardware but are limited in their ability to perform dynamic motions due to the use of high-reduction-ratio servo motors [7–9].

机器人技术的最新进展已使人形机器人能够执行令人瞩目的动态动作，例如行走、高速奔跑和后空翻 [1–4]。然而，许多商用人形机器人是封闭系统，可定制性有限，且难以访问底层传感器和执行器接口。近期的一些成果对软件进行了开源 [5, 6]，而另一些成果虽然提供了开源硬件，但由于采用了大减速比伺服电机，执行动态运动的能力受到限制 [7–9]。

The demand for customizable and high-performance humanoid platforms in the research community is evident. Yet, there remains a scarcity of open-source humanoid hardware capable of performing dynamic locomotion. Such platforms are essential for enabling transparent access, customization, and investigation of low-level hardware interactions, all of which are critical to advancing humanoid research.

研究界对可定制且高性能的人形机器人平台的需求显而易见。然而，能够执行动态运动的开源人形机器人硬件依然匮乏。此类平台对于实现透明访问、定制化以及对底层硬件交互的研究至关重要，而这些对于推动人形机器人研究的进展均不可或缺。

This paper introduces an open-source humanoid platform (Fig. 1), designed to address the lack of open-source hardware capable of dynamic motion. The platform is customizable, allowing researchers to integrate design changes and cooptimize hardware and control systems. Our hardware design mimics human physiology by referencing the ratios of the femur, tibia, foot length, and stance width. The humanoid has a nearly symmetric weight distribution, naturally supporting static straight-knee standing. Additionally, we demonstrate the platform’s capabilities by deploying a reinforcement learning (RL) walking policy, trained in simulation, to the physical system without fine-tuning. Both hardware and software are open-sourced.

Unlike most humanoids that rely on consistent active motor control in locomotion, humans leverage passive dynamics in their gait. Human walking requires minimal muscular effort for leg swinging by utilizing pendulum-like motion [10, 11]. Some bipedal robots use passive dynamics to achieve human-level walking efficiency while requiring little to no power input [12–15]. However, their mass distribution is specifically tuned for passive walking, and any customization, such as adding a payload, may compromise their ability to walk effectively and efficiently. Previous work [12] indicates that straight-knee designs promote efficient walking without active control. These designs can maintain static equilibrium in a standing pose by fully locking the stance leg, while allowing a slight unlock of the knee in the swing leg. Our mechanical design satisfies this criterion through our near-symmetrical hip arrangement.

与大多数在运动中依赖持续主动电机控制的人形机器人不同，人类在步态中利用被动动力学。人类行走通过利用摆动样运动，在腿部摆动时只需极小的肌肉力量 [10, 11]。一些双足机器人利用被动动力学实现了人类级别的行走效率，同时几乎不需要或完全不需要动力输入 [12–15]。然而，它们的质量分布是专门针对被动行走进行调校的，任何定制（例如增加负载）都可能会损害其有效且高效行走的能力。先前的研究 [12] 表明，直膝设计有助于在无主动控制的情况下实现高效行走。这些设计可以通过完全锁定支撑腿来在站立姿态下保持静态平衡，同时允许摆动腿的膝盖轻微解锁。我们的机械设计通过近乎对称的髋关节布局满足了这一准则。

In parallel, research on energy-efficient legged robot control has proposed learning gait patterns [16], using compliant reinforcement learning [17], and optimizing the center-ofmass trajectory [18]. Although end-to-end deep reinforcement learning has achieved state-of-the-art locomotion [19– 21], reinforcement learning based controller design has not yet sufficiently explored incorporating passive dynamics for bipedal locomotion within their framework. While passive dynamics has been widely studied in traditional controller design, its potential application within learning-based controller remains relatively untapped.

与此同时，关于高能效足式机器人控制的研究提出了学习步态模式 [16]、使用顺应性强化学习 [17] 以及优化质心轨迹 [18]。尽管端到端深度强化学习已经实现了最先进的运动性能 [19–21]，但基于强化学习的控制器设计在其框架内尚未充分探索将双足运动的被动动力学纳入其中。虽然被动动力学在传统控制器设计中已得到广泛研究，但其在基于学习的控制器中的潜在应用仍相对未被充分发掘。

II. 相关工作

A. Humanoid Platforms

A. 人形机器人平台

The robotics industry offers several humanoid platforms of various scales [1–4]. However, commercial humanoids often have limited customizability and accessibility due to closedsource hardware. This lack of openness makes it difficult for researchers to modify or experiment with the robot’s low-level systems, limiting opportunities for fundamental research. We address this limitation by offering an opensource platform. Academic labs have developed humanoids for research purposes [5, 6, 8, 22–27]. While these robots may not be as polished or robust as their commercial counterparts, they offer more flexibility in customizing hardware and control, enabling researchers to push the boundaries of hardware and algorithm designs.

机器人行业提供了几种不同规模的人形机器人平台 [1–4]。然而，由于硬件闭源，商用人形机器人通常可定制性和可访问性有限。这种开放性的缺乏使研究人员难以对机器人的底层系统进行修改或实验，从而限制了开展基础研究的机会。我们通过提供一个开源平台来解决这一局限。学术实验室也开发了用于研究目的的人形机器人 [5, 6, 8, 22–27]。虽然这些机器人可能不如商用同类产品完善或稳健，但它们在定制硬件和控制方面提供了更大的灵活性，使研究人员能够突破硬件和算法设计的界限。

C. Strategies for Energy-Efficient Locomotion

C. 高能效运动策略

Hardware Strategies As discussed by Garcia et al. [12], hardware designs for passive dynamic walking often benefit from straight-knee configurations to enable efficient walking without active control. A key design requirement for passive walking is maintaining static equilibrium when standing on flat ground, which can be achieved by fully locking the stance leg while allowing the swing leg’s knee to slightly unlock. Various studies [13–15, 37] have demonstrated the effectiveness of straight-knee designs in achieving fully or mostly passive walking. Additionally, incorporating energy exchange components can enhance energy transmission. For instance, the DURUS robot [38] employs compression springs in the ankles, while the COMAN robot [22] uses compliant joints in the hip, knee, and ankle to store and release energy during locomotion.

硬件策略 正如 Garcia 等人 [12] 所讨论的，被动动态行走的硬件设计通常得益于直膝配置，从而在没有主动控制的情况下实现高效行走。被动行走的一个关键设计要求是在平坦地面上站立时保持静态平衡，这可以通过完全锁定支撑腿，同时允许摆动腿的膝盖轻微解锁来实现。多项研究 [13–15, 37] 证明了直膝设计在实现完全或大部分被动行走方面的有效性。此外，结合能量交换组件可以增强能量传递。例如，DURUS 机器人 [38] 在踝关节中采用压缩弹簧，而 COMAN 机器人 [22] 则在髋部、膝部和踝部使用顺应性关节，以便在运动过程中储存和释放能量。

Control Strategies Energy-efficient control strategies have also been explored. By removing the velocity tracking reward during post-disturbance training, a compliant reinforcement learning policy [17] leads to more energy-efficient push recovery. Similarly, COMAN [18] uses RL to optimize its center-of-mass trajectory for better energy efficiency.

控制策略 高能效控制策略也得到了探索。通过在受扰动后的训练中移除速度跟踪奖励，顺应性强化学习策略 [17] 带来了更高能效的抗推扰恢复能力。类似地，COMAN [18] 使用 RL 优化其质心轨迹以获得更佳的能量效率。

III. METHOD

III. 方法

A. Hardware Design

A. 硬件设计

The Duke Humanoid (Fig. 2) is a child-sized, opensource bipedal robot designed for dynamic locomotion. Standing 1 m from shoulder to foot and weighing 30 kg, it approximately matches the size of an average 8-year-old child [39]. This size was selected for two reasons: first, it allows the robot to interact comfortably with human-centric environment surfaces while standing, enabling future object manipulation tasks; second, its mid-size balances functionality, affordability, and customizability, especially given the high cost of commercially available geared motors.

Duke Humanoid（图 2）是一款儿童体型的开源双足机器人，专为动态运动而设计。从肩部到脚部高 1 m，重 30 kg，其尺寸大致相当于普通 8 岁儿童 [39]。选择该尺寸有两个原因：首先，它使机器人站立时能与以人为中心的环境表面自如交互，从而支持未来的物体操作任务；其次，考虑到市售减速电机的高昂成本，其中型尺寸在功能性、经济性和可定制性之间取得了平衡。

Following a co-design philosophy for complex robotic platforms [40], we iteratively tuned and selected the mechanical design and actuators based on simulations. We designed preliminary humanoid models in Isaac Gym and trained walking policies via reinforcement learning, using the resulting joint torque and velocity data to guide actuator selection.

遵循复杂机器人平台的协同设计理念 [40]，我们基于仿真反复迭代调整并选择了机械设计和执行器。我们在 Isaac Gym 中设计了初步的人形机器人模型，并通过强化学习训练行走策略，利用所得的关节力矩和速度数据来指导执行器的选型。

Mechanical Design Our robot features 10 degrees of freedom (DoFs), with 5 DoFs in each leg: 3 in the hip, 1 for knee flexion and extension (KFE), and 1 for ankle plantar flexion and dorsiflexion. The DoFs for the hip include hip rotation (HR), hip flexion and extension (HFE), and hip abduction and adduction (HAA). The center of mass of the hip is aligned in the frontal plane, allowing the robot to maintain balance and walk with straight knees.

机械设计 我们的机器人具有 10 个自由度（DoF），每条腿有 5 个自由度：髋部 3 个，膝关节屈伸（KFE）1 个，踝关节跖屈与背屈 1 个。髋部的自由度包括髋关节旋转（HR）、髋关节屈伸（HFE）以及髋关节外展与内收（HAA）。髋部的质心在额状面上对齐，使机器人能够保持平衡并以直膝行走。

The proportions of our leg design anatomically match that of a real human. D. Bridging the Sim-to-Real Gap

D. 弥合虚实差距（Sim-to-Real Gap）

Transferring trained RL policies from simulation to the real robot, particularly for complex platforms such as humanoids, remains challenging. Several factors contribute to the sim-to-real gap: Motor backlash can cause discontinuities in actuation, sensor noise introduces uncertainty in observations, and dynamics mismatches, such as differences in joint friction, link mass, and inertia, can change the underlying transition probabilities.

将训练好的RL策略从仿真迁移到真实机器人（尤其是人形机器人等复杂平台）仍然极具挑战性。导致虚实差距的因素有几个：电机侧隙（backlash）可能导致驱动的不连续性，传感器噪声引入了观测的不确定性，而动力学不匹配（例如关节摩擦力、连杆质量和转动惯量的差异）则可能改变底层的状态转移概率。

To bridge these gaps, we first identified critical joint dynamics parameters that are tunable in simulation, including damping, friction, and armature. For each joint, we conducted control frequency sweeps between 1.5-3 Hz to reduce discrepancies between simulation and real-world performance, specifically focusing on joint position, velocity, target position, and torque. This identification process enabled a closer match between the robot’s simulated and real-world behavior.

为了弥合这些差距，我们首先辨识了仿真中可调的关键关节动力学参数，包括阻尼、摩擦力和电枢惯量（armature）。针对每个关节，我们在1.5-3 Hz之间进行了控制频率扫描，以减少仿真与真实世界性能之间的差异，重点关注关节位置、速度、目标位置和扭矩。该辨识过程使机器人在仿真与现实世界中的行为更加吻合。

We also applied domain randomization to increase the robustness of the simulation policy for real-world transfer.

V. CONCLUSION, LIMITATION, AND FUTURE WORK

V. 结论、局限性与未来工作

We introduce the Duke Humanoid v1.0, an open-source hardware and software research platform for studying humanoid locomotion. This paper details our design choices and the development of a baseline reinforcement learning policy for bipedal locomotion. Furthermore, we explore directly modulating joint activation within the end-to-end reinforcement learning framework to leverage passive dynamics for energy-efficient humanoid walking. Preliminary realworld experiments demonstrate that our approach achieved a 31% reduction in the cost of transport.

我们推出了 Duke Humanoid v1.0，这是一个用于研究人形机器人运动的开源软硬件研究平台。本文详细介绍了我们的设计选择，以及用于双足运动的基线强化学习策略的开发。此外，我们探索了在端到端强化学习框架内直接调节关节激活程度，以利用被动动力学实现高能效的人形机器人行走。初步的现实世界实验表明，我们的方法使运输成本（cost of transport）降低了 31%。

However, our platform currently lacks arms, limiting our ability to study whole-body control and mobile manipulation. In the future, we plan to equip the robot with arms and dexterous hands. Our robot is currently tethered, limiting its mobility. We plan on implementing onboard power to study more dynamic motions, such as running and jumping.

然而，我们的平台目前缺少双臂，这限制了我们研究全身控制和移动操作的能力。未来，我们计划为机器人配备手臂和灵巧手。目前我们的机器人采用系缆供电（tethered），限制了其机动性。我们计划实现机载供电，以研究诸如跑步和跳跃等更具动态性的运动。
