I. INTRODUCTION

I. 引言

Humanoid robots hold immense promise for traversing complex real-world environments and executing difficult tasks. Unlike their wheeled counterparts, humanoids can step over obstacles and navigate discontinuous terrain. Recently, the field has witnessed significant strides in humanoid control, enabling robots to perform dynamic maneuvers such as dancing, backflips, and mimicking human motions [22, 24, 27]. However, there is a fundamental distinction between tracking a predefined motion and hiking in the wild. While tracking resembles memorizing a routine, hiking requires the robot to actively perceive the terrain, adapt to irregularities, and handle the unknown.

人形机器人在穿越复杂的现实世界环境和执行高难度任务方面展现出巨大潜力。与轮式机器人不同，人形机器人能够跨越障碍并在不连续的地形中通行。近期，人形机器人控制领域取得了显著进展，使机器人能够执行诸如舞蹈、后空翻以及模仿人体运动等高动态动作 [22, 24, 27]。然而，跟踪预定义的运动与在野外环境中徒步之间存在着本质区别。如果说跟踪类似于死记硬背一套固定动作，那么野外徒步则要求机器人主动感知地形、适应不平整表面并应对未知情况。

Over the past few years, blind locomotion has established a robust baseline [13, 34, 33, 38]. Relying solely on proprioception, these methods are remarkably robust, capable of handling grass or gravel by reacting to contact forces. Yet, the reactive nature of blind locomotion imposes intrinsic limits. Since the robot only responds after a collision, it is vulnerable to significant hazards. Failure to perceive a deep gap or a high step can lead to catastrophic falls. To hike safely, a robot must transition from reactive stability to proactive planning by ”looking ahead.”

在过去的几年里，盲态运动控制（blind locomotion）已经建立起稳固的基准 [13, 34, 33, 38]。这些方法仅依靠本体感受，表现出非凡的稳健性，能够通过对接触力的反应来应对草地或碎石地面。然而，盲态运动的反应式特性带来了固有的局限性。由于机器人只能在发生碰撞后才做出反应，因此极易受到严重危险的威胁。未能感知到较深的沟壑或较高的台阶可能会导致灾难性的摔倒。为了安全徒步，机器人必须通过“前瞻”（looking ahead），从反应式稳定过渡到主动规划。

Integrating exteroceptive perception into locomotion remains a challenge. Existing approaches largely fall into two categories, each with critical bottlenecks. The first category relies on LiDAR to construct elevation maps (2.5D maps) [23, 15] or voxel grids [3], which depend heavily on precise state estimation. In the wild, however, position sensors are prone to drift. Additionally, LiDAR typically has low frequency and suffers from motion distortion, limiting performance in highly dynamic tasks and environments. Other works utilize depth images to reconstruct heightmap [7, 40, 39], which are not scalable on unseen wild environments. They are limited to lowspeed movements on simple structures like planes and stairs. Lastly, due to highly customized configurations (e.g. camera position), the code is often not open-sourced, making these methods difficult to scale and reproduce by the community.

将外感受感知融入运动控制仍然是一项挑战。现有方法大体可分为两类，且各自存在关键瓶颈。第一类依赖 LiDAR 构建高程图（2.5D 地图）[23, 15] 或体素网格（voxel grids）[3]，这些方法严重依赖精确的状态估计。然而在野外环境中，定位传感器极易发生漂移。此外，LiDAR 的采集频率通常较低且存在运动畸变，从而限制了其在高动态任务与环境中的表现。另一类工作则利用深度图像来重建高度图（heightmap）[7, 40, 39]，但这些方法在未见过的野外环境中缺乏可扩展性，仅局限于平面和楼梯等简单结构上的低速运动。最后，由于高度定制化的配置（例如相机安装位置），相关代码往往未开源，导致社区难以对这些方法进行扩展和复现。

To address these challenges, we present Hiking in the Wild , a scalable, end-to-end perceptive parkour framework designed for robust humanoid locomotion in unstructured environments. Unlike complex modular pipelines, our approach leverages a single-stage reinforcement learning scheme that maps raw depth inputs and proprioception directly to joint actions. To handle the high dimensionality of visual data and the complexity of diverse terrain skills, we incorporate a Mixtureof-Experts (MoE) architecture [17, 16]. Our design allows the policy to process depth inputs at high frequencies (up to 60 Hz). A critical yet often overlooked issue in humanoid RL is the precision of foothold placement. Unlike quadrupeds, humanoids are less stable, and stepping partially on an edge (e.g., the lip of a stair) often leads to catastrophic slippage. Previous model-based planners can generate precise footholds but are fragile to map errors [19]. We propose a robust softconstraint mechanism: a geometric Terrain Edge Detector coupled with Volume Points attached to the robot’s feet. Some previous works using virtual obstacles are not scalable [50, 4]. By penalizing the penetration of these volume points with terrain edges during training, the policy implicitly learns to center its feet on safe, flat surfaces, significantly enhancing safety on stairs and gaps without requiring explicit trajectory planning.

人形机器人强化学习中一个关键但常被忽视的问题是落脚点放置的精度。与四足机器人不同，人形机器人的稳定性较差，若脚部部分踩在边缘上（例如楼梯边缘），往往会导致灾难性打滑。以往基于模型的规划器虽然可以生成精确的落脚点，但对建图误差极为脆弱 [19]。我们提出了一种稳健的软约束机制：将几何地形边缘检测器与附加在机器人足部的体积点相结合。以往一些采用虚拟障碍的工作缺乏可扩展性 [50, 4]。通过在训练期间对这些体积点穿透地形边缘的行为进行惩罚，策略隐式地学会将双脚中心落在安全、平坦的表面上，从而在无需显式轨迹规划的情况下，显著提升了在楼梯和沟壑上的安全性。

Furthermore, training robust policies for the wild is complicated by the ”reward hacking” phenomenon, where agents tasked with random velocity commands often learn to spin in place rather than traverse difficult terrain [4]. Some methods using a goal-based command alleviate this problem, but they lack control over speed of the robot [47, 48]. To enforce meaningful exploration, we introduce a Flat Patch Sampling strategy. Instead of arbitrary commands, we identify reachable flat regions in the terrain mesh to serve as feasible navigation targets. Velocity commands are then generated based on the relative position of these patches, with randomized speed limits. This curriculum ensures that the agent is always challenged with physically solvable tasks, accelerating convergence and improving directional compliance.

此外，在野外环境下训练稳健策略还受到“奖励作弊”（reward hacking）现象的困扰：接收随机速度指令的智能体往往学会原地打转，而不是穿越复杂地形 [4]。一些采用基于目标指令的方法虽能缓解该问题，但缺乏对机器人运动速度的控制 [47, 48]。为了引导有意义的探索，我们引入了平坦斑块采样（Flat Patch Sampling）策略。我们不再采用任意指令，而是在地形网格中识别出可达的平坦区域作为可行的导航目标。随后，根据这些斑块的相对位置并结合随机化的速度上限生成速度指令。该课程（curriculum）确保智能体始终面对物理上可解的任务挑战，从而加速收敛并提高方向顺应性。

The robot successfully completed hiking tasks in the wild, robustly traversing stairs, slopes, uneven grassy ground, and discrete gaps. Explicit foothold control typically decouples perception and planning, utilizing terrain representations like elevation maps to solve for optimal placements. Previous research has attempted to solve this by modifying velocity tracking rewards [4, 5] or using goal-based commands [47, 48]. Furthermore, pure goal commands often lack control over the robot’s speed, and randomly sampled goals may not provide suitable targets. To address these issues, we introduce a new method that generates targets using ”flat patches” and creates specific velocity commands to improve training performance.

V. 结论

In this paper, we present a scalable end-to-end perceptive locomotion framework for humanoid robots. By integrating a novel volumetric edge-aware penalization mechanism with position-based velocity command generation, our approach achieves high-dynamic behaviors on complex terrains. We conduct extensive experiments in both simulation and realworld deployment. It demonstrates that our framework enables humanoid robots to perform agile running and stable walking across a variety of challenging environments.

在本文中，我们提出了一种面向人形机器人的可扩展端到端感知运动框架。通过将新颖的基于体积的边缘感知惩罚机制与基于位置的速度指令生成相结合，我们的方法在复杂地形上实现了高动态行为。我们在仿真和真实世界部署中均开展了广泛实验。结果表明，我们的框架能够使人形机器人在各种极具挑战性的环境中实现敏捷奔跑与稳定行走。

Despite these advancements, our current system has two primary limitations. First, the perception system relies solely on a single onboard forward-facing depth camera, which leads to a lack of backward or lateral movement capability. Future work may attach multiple cameras to the robot to enable omnidirectional perception and agility. Second, we observe that training a variety of terrains and gait modes simultaneously can lead to mode collapse and performance degradation compared to specialized policies. Advanced multitask reinforcement learning techniques can be investigated to enhance the capacity of a single unified policy.

尽管取得了这些进展，我们当前的系统仍存在两个主要局限。首先，感知系统完全依赖单个机载前向深度相机，这导致缺乏后向或横向移动能力。未来的工作可以在机器人上加装多台相机，以实现全向感知与敏捷性。其次，我们观察到，与专用策略相比，同时在多种地形和步态模式下进行训练会导致模式坍塌和性能下降。未来可探索先进的多任务强化学习技术，以增强单一统一策略的模型容量。
