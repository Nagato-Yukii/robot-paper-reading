In this paper we show how the task of motion tracking for physically simulated characters can be solved using supervised learning and optimizing a policy directly via back-propagation. To achieve this we make use of a world model trained to approximate a specific subset of the environment’s transition function, effectively acting as a differentiable physics simulator through which the policy can be optimized to minimize the tracking error. Compared to popular model-free methods of physically simulated character control which primarily make use of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) we find direct optimization of the policy via our approach consistently achieves a higher quality of control in a shorter training time, with a reduced sensitivity to the rate of experience gathering, dataset size, and distribution.

本文展示了如何使用监督学习，并通过反向传播直接优化策略，来解决物理仿真角色的动作跟踪任务。为此，我们训练一个世界模型来近似环境转移函数的某个特定子集，使其实际上充当可微物理仿真器，从而通过该模型优化策略，以最小化跟踪误差。与主要采用近端策略优化（PPO）的流行无模型物理仿真角色控制方法相比，我们发现，采用我们的方法直接优化策略，能够持续以更短的训练时间实现更高质量的控制，同时降低对经验采集速率、数据集大小及其分布的敏感性。

1 INTRODUCTION

1 引言

In recent years Reinforcement Learning (RL) has been shown to consistently achieve state-of-the-art results on a large number of physically simulated character control tasks - and the ability to track high quality motion capture data with a physically simulated character is now well within the reach of both animation researchers and practitioners. Many of these works make use of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO), a popular, on-policy Reinforcement Learning algorithm that has been shown to work effectively on a vast range of different problems and applications. However, like many Reinforcement Learning algorithms, PPO is notoriously sample inefficient, sensitive to hyper-parameter settings, class or data imbalance, gym design, reward and feature design, and even the random seed [Henderson et al. 2017]. This makes wide adoption of such methods in areas like video games difficult, as the unpredictability of the algorithm’s success can make it a risky choice in a production setting.

近年来，强化学习（RL）已被证明能够在大量物理仿真角色控制任务中持续取得最先进的结果，而利用物理仿真角色跟踪高质量动作捕捉数据，如今也已成为动画研究者和从业者都能切实实现的能力。其中许多工作采用近端策略优化（PPO），这是一种流行的同策略强化学习算法，已被证明能够有效处理范围广泛的不同问题与应用。然而，与许多强化学习算法一样，PPO 的样本效率低下是出了名的，而且它对超参数设置、类别或数据不平衡、训练环境设计、奖励和特征设计，乃至随机种子都很敏感 [Henderson et al. 2017]。这使得此类方法难以在电子游戏等领域得到广泛采用，因为算法能否成功具有不可预测性，使其在生产环境中成为一种有风险的选择。

In this work we show how the most common task in physically simulated character control - motion tracking - can instead be formulated using only supervised learning such that a control policy can be optimized for directly using back-propagation of a tracking loss. This can be interpreted in two ways: as a form of model-based learning, but also as a supervised learning problem where two models are learned simultaneously - one to predict the dynamics of the world, and another which attempts to produce the optimal actions which minimize the tracking losses. We find our approach achieves a better quality of animation within a shorter training time than PPO, with a reduced sensitivity to the rate of experience gathering and data imbalance, which we show on a large variety of challenging tracking tasks.

在本工作中，我们展示了如何仅使用监督学习来表述物理仿真角色控制中最常见的任务——动作跟踪，从而通过跟踪损失的反向传播直接优化控制策略。这可以从两个角度理解：它既是一种基于模型的学习，也可以视为一个同时学习两个模型的监督学习问题——一个模型预测世界的动力学，另一个模型则尝试生成使跟踪损失最小化的最优动作。我们发现，与 PPO 相比，我们的方法能够以更短的训练时间实现更高质量的动画，同时降低对经验采集速率和数据不平衡的敏感性；我们在多种具有挑战性的跟踪任务上展示了这一点。

2 RELATED WORK

2 相关工作

In this section we give a brief overview of related research including model-based methods, learned world models, model-free methods, and specific works on motion tracking.

本节简要概述相关研究，包括基于模型的方法、学习得到的世界模型、无模型方法，以及专门针对动作跟踪的工作。

2.1 Model-based Learning

2.1 基于模型的学习

Model-based approaches utilize an underlying model of the physical system to achieve motion control. The model allows optimization to be used to solve control problems, either by directly exploiting the structure of the model itself [Brown et al. 2013; Coros et al. 2010; da Silva et al. 2008; Eom et al. 2019; Hong et al. 2019; Jain and Liu 2011; Lee et al. 2010; Macchietto et al. 2009; Muico et al. 2009; Yin et al. 2007], or by using black-box, gradient-free optimization methods [Hämäläinen et al. 2014, 2015; Lee et al. 2014; Liu et al. 2015, 2010; Naderi et al. 2017; Sok et al. 2007]. Given the model, an optimization can be performed either offline or online to find the actions which achieve the desired goals for a specific state. While gradient-free optimization can be applied more generally than optimizations designed for limited, specific domains, it often struggles when the dimensionality of the action space grows.

基于模型的方法利用物理系统的底层模型来实现动作控制。借助模型，可以通过优化来求解控制问题：既可以直接利用模型本身的结构 [Brown et al. 2013; Coros et al. 2010; da Silva et al. 2008; Eom et al. 2019; Hong et al. 2019; Jain and Liu 2011; Lee et al. 2010; Macchietto et al. 2009; Muico et al. 2009; Yin et al. 2007]，也可以使用黑箱无梯度优化方法 [Hämäläinen et al. 2014, 2015; Lee et al. 2014; Liu et al. 2015, 2010; Naderi et al. 2017; Sok et al. 2007]。给定模型后，可以离线或在线执行优化，以找到在特定状态下实现预期目标的动作。尽管与针对受限的特定领域所设计的优化方法相比，无梯度优化的适用范围更广，但随着动作空间维度的增加，它往往会面临困难。

Differentiable models allow gradient-based optimization to be leveraged in a way which scales far better as the number of control parameters increases [Tassa et al. 2012; Todorov et al. 2012], but is hard to formulate beyond specific tasks, and even with gradients, it is not always clear how to use these models to optimize for policies that achieve desired behaviours rather than specific actions limited to individual states [Ding et al. 2015; Liu et al. 2016]. Model-based reinforcement learning is one way to do this, and provides a framework for learning a policy from just a reward signal by optimizing for the control policy that takes actions which maximize the expected discounted return [Janner et al. 2019]. However, model-based

可微模型使基于梯度的优化得以应用，并在控制参数数量增加时展现出好得多的可扩展性 [Tassa et al. 2012; Todorov et al. 2012]，但这种方法很难在特定任务之外加以构建；即使梯度可用，如何利用这些模型来优化能够实现预期行为的策略，而非局限于单个状态下的具体动作，也并非总是清楚的 [Ding et al. 2015; Liu et al. 2016]。基于模型的强化学习是实现这一目标的一种途径，它提供了一个仅依靠奖励信号学习策略的框架：通过优化控制策略，使其采取能够最大化期望折扣回报的动作 [Janner et al. 2019]。然而，基于模型的

reinforcement learning is often just as difficult to apply in the general case. Sometimes models can be designed from first principles using domain expertise [Tassa et al. 2012], or a hybrid approach can be used where an approximate parameterized model is fit to limited data (often called “system identification” [Ljung 1999]). Yet, usually as system complexity grows manual model design becomes impractical.

强化学习在一般情形下往往同样难以应用。有时，可以利用领域专业知识，从第一性原理出发设计模型 [Tassa et al. 2012]；也可以采用混合方法，用有限的数据拟合一个近似的参数化模型（通常称为“系统辨识” [Ljung 1999]）。然而，通常随着系统复杂度的增加，人工设计模型会变得不切实际。

2.2 Learned World Models

2.2 学习得到的世界模型

In an attempt to obtain a differentiable model of a complex system, one approach (and indeed the one proposed in this paper) is to train a generic differentiable model such as a neural network on observations of the system in a supervised way, providing a differentiable approximation which can be used to perform gradient-based optimization of control policies [Nagabandi et al. 2017; Schmidhuber 1990]. These World Models [Ha and Schmidhuber 2018], can take many forms, but are often trained to approximate the transition function of the environment - to predict the next state given the previous state and the action taken [Chiappa et al. 2017; Dosovitskiy and Koltun 2016; Sutton 1991]. This approach, however, comes with its own challenges as many of the elements of the environment may not be included in the observations, may be stochastic in nature, or may change independently of the agent’s actions. In the general case this makes learning the transition function difficult as it must be a probabilistic function [Deisenroth and Rasmussen 2011; Depeweg et al. 2017; Higuera et al. 2018], trained on enough data to break spurious correlations between actions and observations [Wahlström et al. 2015], and handle well the prediction of sparse yet important events [Oh et al. 2015]. In this work we sidestep the majority of these issues by limiting our world model to just the subset of the transition function which is affected by the agent’s actions: the result of the deterministic physics simulation.

为了获得复杂系统的可微模型，一种方法（也正是本文提出的方法）是利用系统的观测数据，以监督学习方式训练神经网络等通用可微模型，从而得到一个可微近似模型，用于对控制策略进行基于梯度的优化 [Nagabandi et al. 2017; Schmidhuber 1990]。这些世界模型 [Ha and Schmidhuber 2018] 可以采用多种形式，但通常通过训练来近似环境的转移函数——即根据前一状态和所采取的动作预测下一状态 [Chiappa et al. 2017; Dosovitskiy and Koltun 2016; Sutton 1991]。然而，这种方法也有其自身的挑战，因为环境中的许多要素可能未被纳入观测，可能本身具有随机性，也可能独立于智能体的动作而变化。在一般情形下，这使得转移函数难以学习，因为它必须是一个概率函数 [Deisenroth and Rasmussen 2011; Depeweg et al. 2017; Higuera et al. 2018]，必须使用足够多的数据进行训练，以消除动作与观测之间的伪相关 [Wahlström et al. 2015]，而且还必须妥善预测稀少但重要的事件 [Oh et al. 2015]。在本工作中，我们将世界模型限定为仅刻画转移函数中受智能体动作影响的子集，即确定性物理仿真的结果，从而避开了上述大部分问题。

Our method can therefore be categorized as a model-based learning method that uses a learned world model. As such, Grzeszczuk et al. [1998] is perhaps the most similar previous work to our approach, as they also train a learned world model to predict the dynamics of an expensive physics simulation. This world model is then used to generate physically-based animation clips using gradient-based trajectory optimization. Our work could be viewed as adding an additional step that attempts to memorize the results of this optimization in the form of the policy network function.

因此，我们的方法可以归类为一种使用学习所得世界模型的基于模型的学习方法。从这一点看，Grzeszczuk et al. [1998] 或许是与我们的方法最为相近的先前工作，因为他们也通过训练世界模型来预测计算成本高昂的物理仿真的动力学。随后，该世界模型被用于通过基于梯度的轨迹优化生成基于物理的动画片段。我们的工作可以看作在此基础上增加了一个步骤，尝试以策略网络函数的形式记忆这一优化过程的结果。

2.3 Model-free Learning

2.3 无模型学习

As with the training of world models, it is often easier to make measurements of a complicated system’s dynamics than it is to design an accurate, flexible model which can be exploited efficiently to build a policy that can achieve the desired behaviors. Model-free reinforcement learning exploits this fact and uses measurements of the response of various states of the environment to various actions to optimize a policy without any knowledge of the underlying dynamics. This makes it attractive for the purposes of solving physical control problems where effects like friction, air resistance, actuator dynamics, contact response, etc. are notoriously difficult to accurately model [Hwangbo et al. 2019]. 2.4 Motion Tracking

2.4 动作跟踪

Many works on motion tracking have focused on the emergence of locomotion-like behaviour from simple objectives or carefully designed heuristics [Coros et al. 2010; Di Carlo et al. 2018; Geijtenbeek and Pronost 2012; Heess et al. 2017; Naderi et al. 2017; Raibert and Hodgins 1991; Sok et al. 2007; Yin et al. 2007]. However, a desire for more realistic animation has brought a focus onto the use of motion capture data [Hong et al. 2019; Liu and Hodgins 2018, 2017; Liu et al. 2016, 2015, 2010], or even video [Peng et al. 2018b], as a tracking reference. The most recent motion tracking works primarily use PPO, and provide a “tracking reward” for how closely the animation data is followed [Bergamin et al. 2019; Chentanez et al. 2018; Luo et al. 2020; Merel et al. 2019b; Park et al. 2019; Peng et al. 2018a, 2021; Won et al. 2020; Yu et al. 2018]. Surprisingly, there is very little work applying model-based RL for motion imitation, with most instances being applied on smaller problems [Zhou et al. 2019], or as benchmarks [Brockman et al. 2016]. To our knowledge, we are the first to apply a learned world model to the problem of motion tracking at such a large scale.

许多动作跟踪研究关注如何通过简单目标或精心设计的启发式方法，使类似行走等位移运动的行为涌现出来 [Coros et al. 2010; Di Carlo et al. 2018; Geijtenbeek and Pronost 2012; Heess et al. 2017; Naderi et al. 2017; Raibert and Hodgins 1991; Sok et al. 2007; Yin et al. 2007]。然而，对更逼真动画的追求使研究重点转向使用动作捕捉数据 [Hong et al. 2019; Liu and Hodgins 2018, 2017; Liu et al. 2016, 2015, 2010]，甚至视频 [Peng et al. 2018b] 作为跟踪参考。最新的动作跟踪研究主要使用 PPO，并根据对动画数据的跟随程度提供“跟踪奖励” [Bergamin et al. 2019; Chentanez et al. 2018; Luo et al. 2020; Merel et al. 2019b; Park et al. 2019; Peng et al. 2018a, 2021; Won et al. 2020; Yu et al. 2018]。令人意外的是，将基于模型的强化学习用于动作模仿的研究非常少，其中大多数应用于规模较小的问题 [Zhou et al. 2019]，或将其作为基准测试 [Brockman et al. 2016]。据我们所知，我们首次将学习得到的世界模型应用于如此大规模的动作跟踪问题。

3.1 Environment

3.1 环境

To allow for enough data to be collected we set up a training environment that closely resembles an RL gym similar in style to that of DeepMimic [Peng et al. 2018a]. We simulate 256 different articulated characters in parallel with motors at each joint, driven by PD controllers, with the goal of tracking some corresponding kinematic animation taken from a motion capture database. Whenever some maximum episode length is exceeded, or some minimum episode length has been obtained and the character’s head height has deviated from the reference by more than 25cm, reference state initialization [Peng et al. 2018a] is used to reset the simulated character to a random pose from a random animation in the kinematic animation database, with vertical correction applied to avoid ground penetrations. We use a maximum episode length of 512, a minimum episode length of 48, and provide an option to apply resets with respect to some user-provided probability distribution over animations. Once an episode terminates, the samples from the environment are added to a large cyclic data buffer (see Section 3.5 for more details). This setup generates data at a rate of \(\sim 5000\) samples per second. For more details on our simulation settings please see Section 5.3.

为采集足够的数据，我们搭建了一个训练环境，它与强化学习训练场（RL gym）十分相似，风格类似于 DeepMimic [Peng et al. 2018a] 所使用的环境。我们并行仿真 256 个不同的关节式角色，每个关节均配有由 PD 控制器驱动的电机，目标是跟踪从动作捕捉数据库中取得的相应运动学动画。当回合长度超过设定的最大值，或已达到设定的最小回合长度且角色头部高度相对参考值的偏差超过 25cm 时，我们采用参考状态初始化 [Peng et al. 2018a]，将仿真角色重置为运动学动画数据库中随机选取的某段动画中的随机姿态，并进行竖直方向的修正，以避免穿透地面。我们采用的最大回合长度为 512，最小回合长度为 48，并提供一个选项，允许按照用户给定的动画概率分布执行重置。每当一个回合终止，环境中的样本就会被加入一个大型循环数据缓冲区（更多细节见第 3.5 节）。该配置每秒约生成 \(5000\) 个样本。关于仿真设置的更多细节，请参见第 5.3 节。

3.2 Representation

3.2 表示

To represent the state of a given character \(\mathbf{X}\) we use the world space positions \(\mathbf{x}^{p} \in \mathbb{R}^{3B}\), velocities \(\dot{\mathbf{x}}^{p} \in \mathbb{R}^{3B}\), rotations \(\mathbf{x}^{r} \in \mathbb{R}^{4B}\), and rotational velocities \(\dot{\mathbf{x}}^{r} \in \mathbb{R}^{3B}\) of all the associated rigid bodies, \(\mathbf{X} = \{\mathbf{x}^{p}\;\dot{\mathbf{x}}^{p}\;\mathbf{x}^{r}\;\dot{\mathbf{x}}^{r}\}\), where \(B\) is the number of rigid bodies. Here, rotations are represented as quaternions. We use the same representation for both the kinematic character \(\mathbf{K}\) and the simulated character \(\mathbf{S}\).

To represent the simulated character’s PD targets \(\mathbf{T}\) we use the target rotation \(\mathbf{t} \in \mathbb{R}^{4J}\) and target rotational velocity \(\dot{\mathbf{t}} \in \mathbb{R}^{3J}\) for each joint \(\mathbf{T} = \{\mathbf{t}\;\dot{\mathbf{t}}\}\), where \(J\) is the number of joints. These are multiplied by the joint rotations of the kinematic character \(\mathbf{k}^{t} \in \mathbb{R}^{4J}\) to get the final PD targets \(\mathbf{t} = \exp\left(\frac{\alpha}{2}\mathbf{o}\right) \otimes \mathbf{k}^{t}\), where \(\alpha\) provides a way to adjust the overall scaling of the offsets. Since our method only applies offsets to the target rotations, the PD target rotational velocities \(\dot{\mathbf{t}}\) are simply copied over directly from the rotational velocities of the joints of the kinematic character \(\dot{\mathbf{k}}^{t} \in \mathbb{R}^{3J}\), \(\dot{\mathbf{t}} = \dot{\mathbf{k}}^{t}\) or set to zero in physics simulators which do not support them \(\dot{\mathbf{t}} = 0\). 5.3 Performance

5.3 性能

In general our method has similar performance characteristics to existing methods of physically simulated character control using PPO. Policy network evaluation is performed every frame, takes \(\sim 1800\,\mu\mathrm{s}\), and requires \(\sim 20\,\mathrm{MB}\) of storage for the network weights. World model performance and memory usage is similar. We run our physics simulations at 240 Hz, with 20 iterations of the constraint solver, which typically takes between \(300\,\mu\mathrm{s}\) and \(1000\,\mu\mathrm{s}\) per-frame depending on the simulator used. All performance tests were run on an Intel Xeon E5-1650 CPU.

6.1 Window Size

6.1 窗口大小

A window-based training scheme allows a model to learn longer-range predictions in a more stable way than single-step predictions. For the policy training, a larger window is needed for learning motions where long-term apprehension is required, such as taking a step in a walk cycle. However, the quality of the policy’s actions over these longer-term horizons relies on the world model’s long term prediction accuracy. Meanwhile, having a world model training window size that is too large can decouple the predicted states from the target states which can cause the world model to ignore the actions taken. Fig 15 demonstrates the effect of changing the relative training window size of the world model and policy. If we set the policy window to 64 the training becomes unstable and terminates. We found that a policy window of 32 and world model window of 8 were consistently stable across all the tasks we tried.

与单步预测相比，基于窗口的训练方案能够让模型以更稳定的方式学习更长时间范围的预测。在策略训练中，对于需要长时程预判的动作，例如在步行周期中迈出一步，需要使用较大的窗口来学习。然而，策略在这些较长时间范围内所采取动作的质量，依赖于世界模型的长期预测精度。同时，世界模型的训练窗口过大可能使预测状态与目标状态脱节，从而导致世界模型忽略所采取的动作。图 15 展示了改变世界模型与策略的相对训练窗口大小所产生的影响。如果将策略窗口设为 64，训练就会变得不稳定并终止。我们发现，在所尝试的所有任务中，将策略窗口设为 32、世界模型窗口设为 8，均能保持稳定。

8 DISCUSSION AND FUTURE WORK

8 讨论与未来工作

While PPO utilizes empirical estimates of the gradients to optimize the policy, our approach computes an analytical gradient. This has both pros and cons - on the one hand we are capable of achieving accurate tracking for all rigid bodies and their velocities since our policy always moves in the direction which best optimizes all the different loss components together. Empirically our method also seems to scale better to larger network sizes. On the other hand, our method does not exploit exploration in the same way as PPO, and it can be more difficult for it to discover novel behaviours such as stepping motions to recover from falls, as these kinds of behaviours do not typically lie on a direct downward gradient from the current policy and can require a more exploratory search method to discover. This means artifacts such as foot sliding can appear in some motions where the short-horizon motion tracking loss is not able to plan as far ahead as PPO’s longer-horizon returns.

PPO 利用梯度的经验估计来优化策略，而我们的方法计算解析梯度。这既有优点，也有缺点。一方面，我们能够对所有刚体及其速度实现精确跟踪，因为我们的策略始终沿着能够对所有不同损失分量进行最佳联合优化的方向更新。从实验结果来看，我们的方法似乎也能更好地扩展到规模更大的网络。另一方面，我们的方法并不像 PPO 那样利用探索，因此可能更难发现新颖行为，例如用于从跌倒中恢复的迈步动作，因为这类行为通常无法通过从当前策略直接沿梯度下降方向更新来获得，而可能需要探索性更强的搜索方法才能发现。这意味着，在某些动作中，短时域动作跟踪损失无法像 PPO 的较长时域回报那样进行长远规划，因此可能出现脚部滑动等不自然现象。

Stable and accurate world model predictions are necessary for our method to be successful. In order to achieve this in the high dimensional state space it’s important to use a translation and rotation-invariant state representation as well as a window-based training scheme. Similarly, by limiting the world model to learning only the subspace of possible dynamics close to the kinematic character’s movements we can significantly simplify the learning task. The stability of long-term predictions could potentially be improved in the future by additionally learning a compressed, latent, encoding of the character state and performing the physical integration in this space.

稳定且准确的世界模型预测是我们的方法取得成功的必要条件。为了在高维状态空间中实现这一点，采用具有平移和旋转不变性的状态表示以及基于窗口的训练方案十分重要。同样，通过将世界模型的学习范围限制在接近运动学角色动作的可能动力学子空间内，我们可以显著简化学习任务。未来还可以额外学习角色状态的压缩潜在编码，并在该空间中进行物理积分，从而有望改善长期预测的稳定性。

We found that during inference it’s possible to use our world model as an approximate replacement of the real physics simulation it is trained on. However, in our case this is often more expensive than the original simulation due to the relatively large network size.

我们发现，在推理过程中，可以使用我们的世界模型近似替代为其提供训练数据的真实物理仿真。然而，在我们的设置中，由于网络规模相对较大，这样做的计算开销往往高于原始仿真。

9 CONCLUSION

9 结论

In this paper we present a method for motion tracking of physically simulated characters that uses supervised learning to produce a differentiable world model. This allows control policies to learn optimal actions directly via gradient-descent by minimizing the tracking losses over finite horizons. We present our results on a number of difficult and varied tracking tasks, and provide an in-depth comparison to PPO, evaluating the training time, motion quality, and sensitivity to various hyper-parameter settings and other high level design decisions.

本文提出了一种用于物理仿真角色的动作跟踪方法，通过监督学习构建可微世界模型。这使控制策略能够通过最小化有限时域内的跟踪损失，直接利用梯度下降学习最优动作。我们展示了该方法在多种高难度跟踪任务上的结果，并与 PPO 进行了深入比较，评估了训练时间、动作质量，以及对各种超参数设置和其他高层设计决策的敏感性。
