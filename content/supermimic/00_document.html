Abstract: Tracking large human-motion datasets has become the dominant route to general, expressive humanoid whole-body control, but scaling across many motions is expensive. Current pipelines use model-free reinforcement learning, either merging per-motion specialists through lossy distillation, or brute-forcing a single policy with cluster-scale compute. SuperMimic amortizes learning across all motions with one differentiable world model of their shared dynamics, fit from logged transitions of a standard rigid-body simulator. The world model and the tracking policy train together in one loop, and backpropagation-through-time over short imagined rollouts gives the policy analytic gradients. Fast imagined rollouts and analytic gradients make policy learning efficient. In 3 hours on a single GPU, SuperMimic surpasses the return of a state-of-the-art motion tracker baseline trained for 7 days on 128 GPUs. It reduces GPU-hours by more than \(1000\times\) and fails on only 25 of 129,514 benchmark motions, versus 179 failures for the baseline. After 9.8 hours on two GPUs, SuperMimic achieves zero failures. From 1 to 264 hours of motion, the world model’s prediction error stays stable, and GPU-hours to a target return grow slowly. Our policy transfers to a physical Unitree G1 across diverse and acrobatic motions. SuperMimic removes compute as the bottleneck to scaling humanoid whole-body control, making it practical to measure motion-tracking scaling laws.

摘要：跟踪大规模人体运动数据集已成为实现通用、高表现力的人形机器人全身控制的主流途径，但在大量运动上进行扩展成本高昂。现有流程采用无模型强化学习，要么通过有损蒸馏将针对单一运动的专家策略进行合并，要么依靠集群级算力暴力训练单一策略。SuperMimic 利用一个对其共享动力学进行建模的可微世界模型来摊销所有运动上的学习，该世界模型拟合自标准刚体仿真器记录的转移数据。世界模型与跟踪策略在同一个循环中联合训练，短时想象轨迹展开上的沿时间反向传播为策略提供了解析梯度。快速的想象轨迹展开与解析梯度使策略学习极为高效。在单块 GPU 上训练 3 小时，SuperMimic 的回报便超过了在 128 块 GPU 上训练 7 天的代表性运动跟踪器基线。它将 GPU 小时数减少了 \(1000\times\) 以上，在包含 129,514 个基准运动中仅失败 25 个，而基线有 179 个失败。在两块 GPU 上训练 9.8 小时后，SuperMimic 实现了零失败。从 1 小时到 264 小时的运动数据规模下，世界模型的预测误差保持稳定，且达到目标回报所需的 GPU 小时数增长缓慢。我们的策略成功迁移到了真实的 Unitree G1 机器人上，能够完成多样化及特技动作。SuperMimic 消除了阻碍人形机器人全身控制扩展的算力瓶颈，使测定运动跟踪的扩展律成为现实。

1 Introduction

1 引言

policies to track reference motion clips [2] drawn from large motion-capture (mocap) datasets [1]. Existing systems scale this idea either by distilling many clip-specific experts into one controller [3] or by training a generalist policy over a large motion library [1]. In both cases, a more diverse motion library produces a more capable controller. But both also become computationally expensive as motion coverage grows: the state-of-the-art tracker SONIC [1], for example, trains for \(\sim 168\) h on 128 GPUs, totaling roughly 21,000 GPU-hours. Although reference motions differ, they share the same humanoid hybrid rigid-body dynamics. Model-free RL fails to explicitly model this shared structure, instead repeatedly estimating action consequences from noisy simulator rollouts. Scaling to more motions therefore amounts to scaling up compute.

策略来跟踪来自大规模动作捕捉（mocap）数据集 [1] 的参考运动片段 [2]。现有系统在扩展该思想时，要么通过将多个针对特定动作片段的专家策略蒸馏为单一控制器 [3]，要么在庞大的运动库上训练通用策略 [1]。在这两种情况下，更多样化的运动库都会产生能力更强的控制器。但随着运动覆盖范围的扩大，两者的计算开销都变得十分昂贵：例如，最先进的跟踪器 SONIC [1] 在 128 块 GPU 上训练了约 168 小时（\(\sim 168\) h），总计耗费约 21,000 个 GPU 小时。尽管参考运动各不相同，但它们共享相同的人形机器人混合刚体动力学。无模型强化学习未能对这种共享结构进行显式建模，而是反复通过带有噪声的仿真器轨迹展开来估计动作后果。因此，扩展到更多运动就等同于扩大计算规模。

SuperMimic addresses this inefficiency with a single learned, differentiable world model fit from logged simulator transitions. Backpropagation-through-time (BPTT) through short imagined rollouts provides analytic policy gradients through the learned model, allowing one dynamics model to amortize learning across the entire mocap library. Because these gradients come from the learned model rather than from differentiating the simulator, SuperMimic remains compatible with rigidbody simulators, including those with non-smooth contacts and non-differentiable implementations.

SuperMimic 利用拟合自记录仿真器转移数据的单个学习得到的可微世界模型来解决这一低效问题。通过短时想象轨迹展开的沿时间反向传播（BPTT）借助学习到的模型提供解析策略梯度，使得单一动力学模型能够摊销整个动捕库上的学习过程。由于这些梯度来自学习得到的模型而非直接微分仿真器，SuperMimic 依然与刚体仿真器兼容，包括具有非光滑接触和不可微实现的仿真器。

On one GPU, SuperMimic surpasses the normalized return of SONIC’s released checkpoint within 3 h and reduces GPU-hours by more than \(1000\times\) (§4.1). On a 129,514-motion benchmark, SuperMimic fails on 25 motions after 3 h on one GPU and on none after 9.8 h on two GPUs, against 179 for SONIC’s released checkpoint (Appendix F). Across a \(264\times\) increase in dataset length, the world model’s prediction error stays stable and SuperMimic’s compute to a fixed return grows slowly. The learned policy transfers to a physical Unitree G1. In a preliminary study of downstream interfaces, an RL-trained velocity encoder outperforms a distillation baseline.

在单块 GPU 上，SuperMimic 在 3 小时内便超越了 SONIC 公开发布检查点的归一化回报，并将 GPU 小时数减少了 \(1000\times\) 以上（§4.1）。在包含 129,514 个运动的基准测试中，SuperMimic 在单块 GPU 上训练 3 小时后仅在 25 个运动上失败，在两块 GPU 上训练 9.8 小时后实现零失败，相比之下 SONIC 发布的检查点失败了 179 个（附录 F）。在数据集长度增加 \(264\times\) 的情况下，世界模型的预测误差保持稳定，且 SuperMimic 达到固定回报所需的计算量增长缓慢。所学策略成功迁移到了实体 Unitree G1 上。在对下游接口的初步研究中，通过强化学习训练的速度编码器性能优于蒸馏基线。

Contributions.

主要贡献。

- Model-based RL (MBRL) for deployable humanoid control . We demonstrate that BPTT through a learned, differentiable world model can train whole-body tracking policies in a setting long dominated by model-free proximal policy optimization (PPO) [4], and that the resulting controller transfers to a physical Unitree G1.

- 可部署人形机器人控制的基于模型的强化学习（MBRL）。我们证明了在长期由无模型近端策略优化（PPO）[4] 主导的领域中，通过学习得到的可微世界模型进行沿时间反向传播（BPTT）能够训练全身跟踪策略，且所得控制器可迁移至实体 Unitree G1 机器人上。

- Compute is no longer the bottleneck to scaling. SuperMimic surpasses the return of SONIC, a cluster-scale PPO pipeline, on a single GPU and reduces GPU-hours by more than \(1000\times\) (§4.1). Its training cost grows slowly as the motion library expands, making scaling humanoid wholebody control practical without huge GPU infrastructure.

- 算力不再是扩展的瓶颈。SuperMimic 在单块 GPU 上的回报超过了集群规模的 PPO 流程 SONIC，并将 GPU 小时数减少了 \(1000\times\) 以上（§4.1）。随着运动库的扩展，其训练成本增长缓慢，使得无需庞大的 GPU 基础设施即可切实扩展人形机器人全身控制。

- An analysis of the ingredients behind the gains. We identify three world-model properties behind the compute reduction: prediction error that stays stable over a \(264\times\) increase in dataset length, rollouts more than \(10\times\) faster than the simulator, and analytic gradients, with which the policy reaches the best return of PPO on the world model with about \(64\times\) fewer imagined steps.

- 收益背后核心要素的剖析。我们确定了计算量减少背后的三个世界模型特性：数据集长度增加 \(264\times\) 时预测误差依然保持稳定；轨迹展开速度比仿真器快 \(10\times\) 以上；以及解析梯度使得策略仅需减少约 \(64\times\) 的想象步数即可达到 PPO 在世界模型上的最佳回报。

- RL-trained downstream control beyond distillation. As a preliminary study, the same learned world model trains a downstream goal encoder on the interface’s own task reward. On velocity commands, the policy tracks more accurately with this encoder than with a distillation baseline.

- 超越蒸馏的强化学习训练下游控制。作为一项初步研究，同一个学习到的世界模型可在接口自身的任务奖励下训练下游目标编码器。在速度指令控制下，该策略配备此编码器时的跟踪精度高于蒸馏基线。

2 Related Work

2 相关工作

Large-scale physics-based motion tracking. Physics-based motion imitation learns controllers that reproduce reference motions under contact-rich rigid-body dynamics. DeepMimic [2] demonstrated expressive character skills through model-free reinforcement learning, and PHC [11] extended this paradigm to large motion sets with robust recovery. SONIC [1] further demonstrated that scaling motion data, policy capacity, and training compute produces generalist controllers that transfer to humanoid hardware. These results establish motion tracking as a practical route to broad wholebody behavior coverage. However, training these large-scale motion trackers requires extensive simulator rollouts and substantial computation. SuperMimic addresses this bottleneck by learning a differentiable dynamics model shared across all motions, so policy improvement can exploit analytic gradients rather than model-free rollout estimates.

大规模基于物理的运动跟踪。基于物理的运动模仿旨在学习在富接触刚体动力学下复现参考动作的控制器。DeepMimic [2] 通过无模型强化学习展示了富有表现力的角色技能，PHC [11] 将这一范式扩展到具有鲁棒恢复能力的大规模动作集。SONIC [1] 进一步证明，扩展动作数据、策略容量和训练算力能够产生可迁移至人形机器人硬件的通用控制器。这些成果确立了运动跟踪作为实现广泛全身行为覆盖的可行途径。然而，训练这些大规模运动跟踪器需要大量仿真器轨迹展开和庞大计算量。SuperMimic 通过学习跨所有动作共享的可微动力学模型来解决这一瓶颈，使策略改进能够利用解析梯度而非无模型的轨迹展开估计。

Learned differentiable dynamics for motion control. SuperTrack [12] optimizes motion-control policies through a learned differentiable surrogate of the simulator. ControlVAE [6] jointly learns a dynamics model, latent skill representation, and generative controller, while MoConVQ [13] extends learned-model training to discrete motion representations over tens of hours of data. SuperDyno [14] scales differentiable neural dynamics to tens of thousands of motion clips. These methods establish the effectiveness of learned dynamics and analytic policy gradients for scalable simulated-character control. SuperMimic extends this direction to single-GPU training of a large-scale humanoid tracker and to its sim-to-real deployment: its world model conditions on simulator context, while the deployed actor uses noisy proprioceptive observations and a goal input (§3.2). We evaluate how model accuracy, rollout throughput, and differentiable policy updates contribute to training efficiency, and how prediction error behaves as motion coverage grows (§4.1–4.2), and demonstrate physical deployment of the resulting controller (§4.4).

用于运动控制的学习可微动力学。SuperTrack [12] 通过仿真器的学习可微代理来优化运动控制策略。ControlVAE [6] 联合学习动力学模型、潜在技能表征和生成式控制器，而 MoConVQ [13] 将学习模型训练扩展到数十小时数据上的离散运动表征。SuperDyno [14] 将可微神经动力学扩展到数万个运动片段。这些方法确立了学习动力学和解析策略梯度在可扩展的仿真角色控制中的有效性。SuperMimic 将这一方向拓展到大规模人形机器人跟踪器的单 GPU 训练及其仿真到现实迁移部署中：其世界模型以仿真环境上下文为条件，而部署的行动者则使用带有噪声的本体感受观测和目标输入（§3.2）。我们评估了模型精度、轨迹展开吞吐量和可微策略更新对训练效率的贡献，以及预测误差随动作覆盖范围扩大的变化规律（§4.1–4.2），并展示了所得控制器的物理实体部署（§4.4）。

Model-based policy optimization and robot deployment. Dreamer [15] and DreamerV3 [16] learn latent dynamics for imagination-based actor–critic learning, while TD-MPC [17] combines short-horizon planning with terminal-value prediction. DayDreamer [18] demonstrates world-model learning directly on physical robots. SHAC [19] uses differentiable simulation for short-horizon actor–critic updates, motivating SuperMimic’s critic-bootstrapped objective. Analytic-gradient robot control can also combine a simplified differentiable surrogate with a high-fidelity forward simulator, as demonstrated for quadruped locomotion [20]. SuperMimic learns its surrogate from simulator transitions and backpropagates through short imagined rollouts, retaining compatibility with standard rigid-body simulators.

基于模型的策略优化与机器人部署。Dreamer [15] 和 DreamerV3 [16] 学习潜在动力学以进行基于想象的行动者-价值网络学习，而 TD-MPC [17] 将短时界规划与终端价值预测相结合。DayDreamer [18] 展示了直接在真实物理机器人上学习世界模型。SHAC [19] 使用可微仿真进行短时界行动者-价值网络更新，启发了 SuperMimic 的价值网络自举目标。解析梯度机器人控制还可以将简化的可微代理与高保真正向仿真器相结合，四足机器人的运动控制已证明了这一点 [20]。SuperMimic 从仿真器转移中学习其代理模型，并通过短时想象轨迹展开进行反向传播，保持了与标准刚体仿真器的兼容性。

RWM [21] and RWM-U [22] demonstrate that policies trained in learned neural simulators can transfer to physical robots. Both optimize policies using PPO on imagined rollouts; RWM-U additionally uses uncertainty-penalized offline optimization. SuperMimic instead optimizes the policy by differentiating the return through its learned dynamics. RWM and RWM-U pretrain the world model on trajectories from other policies. In contrast, SuperMimic fits its world model during training on data its own policy collects.

RWM [21] 和 RWM-U [22] 证明了在学习得到的神经仿真器中训练的策略可以迁移到物理机器人。两者都在想象轨迹展开上使用 PPO 优化策略；RWM-U 还额外使用了不确定性惩罚的离线优化。相比之下，SuperMimic 通过在其学习的动力学中微分回报来优化策略。RWM 和 RWM-U 在其他策略的轨迹上预训练世界模型；相反，SuperMimic 则在训练过程中使用自身策略收集的数据拟合其世界模型。

3 Method

3 方法

3.1 SuperMimic training loop

3.1 SuperMimic 训练循环

SuperMimic trains one goal-conditioned whole-body controller across a multi-skill motion library and supports multiple goal interfaces. It captures the shared humanoid dynamics in a differentiable world model and improves the policy through short imagined rollouts. Training alternates between three phases in one loop (Figure 2). First, the current policy collects simulator transitions into a replay buffer. Second, we fit the differentiable world model to short replay windows. Third, the policy is improved by backpropagating an imagined return through the frozen world model, with each rollout seeded from a replay-buffer state in the Dyna style [23, 24]. The buffer, model, and policy are updated throughout training, so dynamics learning and control improve together rather than in separate stages (Algorithm A.1, Appendix D). Transitions from earlier policies remain valid for fitting the world model, because the dynamics do not depend on the policy that collected them.

SuperMimic 跨多技能动作库训练一个目标条件全身控制器，并支持多种目标接口。它在可微世界模型中捕捉共享的人形机器人动力学，并通过简短的假想轨迹展开来改进策略。训练在一个循环中的三个阶段之间交替进行（图 2）。首先，当前策略在仿真中收集转移并存入重放缓冲区。其次，我们将可微世界模型拟合到简短的重放窗口。第三，通过冻结的世界模型反向传播假想回报来改进策略，每次轨迹展开都以 Dyna 风格从重放缓冲区状态开始播种 [23, 24]。缓冲区、模型和策略在整个训练过程中不断更新，因此动力学学习和控制是共同提升的，而不是分阶段进行的（算法 A.1，附录 D）。早期策略产生的转移对于拟合世界模型仍然有效，因为动力学并不依赖于收集它们的策略。

3.3 Learning the policy through the world model

During each policy update, we hold the world-model weights \(\omega\) fixed and optimize \(\pi_\theta\) and \(V_\psi\) on imagined rollouts of \(H = 32\) steps. The critic \(V_\psi\) estimates the return beyond step \(H\), so the model must be accurate only within these \(H\) steps. This imagination horizon exceeds the \(k\)-step replay windows on which we fit the model (§3.2). In practice, the learned model remains stable over indefinitely long rollouts, because the \(k\)-step autoregressive fit trains it to correct its own prediction errors.

在每次策略更新期间，我们保持世界模型权重 \(\omega\) 固定，并在 \(H = 32\) 步的假想轨迹展开上优化 \(\pi_\theta\) 和 \(V_\psi\)。评价器 \(V_\psi\) 估计超出第 \(H\) 步之后的回报，因此模型仅需在这 \(H\) 步内保持准确。这一假想视界超过了我们拟合模型所用的 \(k\) 步重放窗口（§3.2）。在实践中，学习到的模型在无限长的轨迹展开中仍能保持稳定，因为 \(k\) 步自回归拟合训练了它纠正自身预测误差的能力。

The policy and critic updates together form a short-horizon actor–critic in the spirit of SHAC [19], which backpropagates through a differentiable simulator. SuperMimic replaces SHAC’s hard episode termination with the survival weight \(\hat{c}_t\), and its critic has no exponential-moving-average target network. Prior motion-tracking controllers trained with analytic gradients often omit a critic and optimize only the \(H\)-step reward sum [12, 6]. Without a critic, training can reach a similar nominal tracking return, but this unbootstrapped objective is more myopic. The critic matters after perturbations, where recovery or balance rewards can lie beyond the imagination window (§4.3).

策略和评价器的更新共同构成了借鉴 SHAC [19] 思想的短视界 Actor-Critic 结构，后者通过可微仿真器进行反向传播。SuperMimic 用存活权重 \(\hat{c}_t\) 替换了 SHAC 的硬回合终止，且其评价器没有指数移动平均目标网络。先前通过解析梯度训练的动作跟踪控制器通常省略了评价器，仅优化 \(H\) 步奖励和 [12, 6]。在没有评价器的情况下，训练可以达到类似的标称跟踪回报，但这种未经自举的目标更为短视。在受到扰动后，评价器起着关键作用，因为恢复或平衡奖励可能落在假想窗口之外（§4.3）。

4 Experimental Results

4 实验结果

We show that SuperMimic scales favorably relative to PPO pipelines, identify the world-model properties behind this scaling, demonstrate transfer on a real Unitree G1, and report a preliminary study of downstream goal interfaces. The simulator is MuJoCo Warp, a GPU implementation of MuJoCo [26]. All our reported costs are measured on NVIDIA H100 (80 GB SXM5) GPUs of Lambda on-demand instances. We ran several random seeds and found training very stable, which we attribute to the dense reward that motion tracking gives at every step. The figures therefore do not report seed variation. Normalized return, return for short, is the undiscounted sum of the per-step reward over the full episode, divided by the largest value this sum can take. A policy that terminates early therefore scores low.

4.1 SuperMimic reaches SONIC-level tracking with far fewer GPU-hours

4.1 SuperMimic 以大幅减少的 GPU 工时达到 SONIC 水平的跟踪性能

We compare against SONIC, a published PPO pipeline trained on a 128-GPU cluster. Both methods build on the BeyondMimic motion-tracking formulation [3], making SONIC the closest available published comparison. Within 3 h on one GPU, SuperMimic surpasses the normalized return of the released SONIC checkpoint (Figure 3(a)). On a shared benchmark of 129,514 motions, it also fails on fewer motions than SONIC: 25 after 3 h on one GPU and 0 after 9.8 h on two GPUs, against 179 for SONIC’s released checkpoint (Appendix F). We state the 3 h conservatively: from then on, every checkpoint is ahead of SONIC by at least half of the gap between SONIC and SuperMimic’s best result, in both return and failed motions. SuperMimic thus uses more than \(50\times\) less wall-clock time and more than \(1000\times\) fewer GPU-hours than SONIC.

我们与 SONIC 进行了对比，后者是在 128 块 GPU 集群上训练的已发表 PPO 管线。这两种方法均建立在 BeyondMimic 动作跟踪表述 [3] 之上，使 SONIC 成为最接近的可比已发表工作。在单块 GPU 上训练 3 小时内，SuperMimic 就超过了发布的 SONIC 检查点的归一化回报（图 3(a)）。在包含 129,514 个动作的共享基准上，它的失败动作数也少于 SONIC：单块 GPU 训练 3 小时后仅失败 25 个动作，两块 GPU 训练 9.8 小时后失败 0 个，而 SONIC 发布的检查点失败 179 个（附录 F）。我们将 3 小时的说法保守表述：从那时起，每个检查点在回报和失败动作数两方面都领先 SONIC 至少达到 SONIC 与 SuperMimic 最佳结果之间差距的一半。因此，SuperMimic 比 SONIC 减少了超过 \(50\times\) 的实际运行时间以及超过 \(1000\times\) 的 GPU 小时数。

Compute accounting. The ratios compare SONIC’s reported 128 NVIDIA L40 GPUs for 7 days (about 21,000 GPU-hours) with 3 h on one NVIDIA H100, within which SuperMimic surpasses the return of SONIC’s released checkpoint. They are about \(56\times\) (\(>50\times\)) in wall-clock time and about \(7200\times\) (\(>1000\times\)) in GPU-hours. The GPU models also differ: in peak throughput, an H100 delivers \(0.74\times\) the fp32 and \(5.5\times\) the tensor-core performance of an L40; how much of that peak either run used is unknown. Nonetheless, \(2.3\times\) in data and at most \(5.5\times\) in peak GPU throughput cannot account for a \(1000\times\) gap in GPU-hours.

计算核算。这些比率是将 SONIC 所报告的使用 128 块 NVIDIA L40 GPU 训练 7 天（约 21,000 个 GPU 小时），与在单块 NVIDIA H100 上训练 3 小时（在此期间 SuperMimic 超过了 SONIC 发布的检查点的回报）进行对比。它们在实际运行时间上大约相差 \(56\times\)（\(>50\times\)），在 GPU 小时数上大约相差 \(7200\times\)（\(>1000\times\)）。这 3 小时涵盖了完整的训练循环；检查点评估在训练后运行，不计入在内。SuperMimic 在来自 BONES-SEED [27] 的 264.56 小时动作上进行训练，该数据集是 SONIC 数据的公开子集，比 SONIC 的 611 小时约少 \(2.3\times\)。GPU 型号也不同：在峰值吞吐量方面，H100 提供 L40 的 \(0.74\times\) fp32 性能和 \(5.5\times\) Tensor Core 性能；两者的运行各自利用了多少峰值性能尚不得而知。尽管如此，数据量上的 \(2.3\times\) 和峰值 GPU 吞吐量上至多 \(5.5\times\) 的差异，无法解释 GPU 小时数上 \(1000\times\) 的差距。

PPO-Sim shares SuperMimic’s motions, observations, reward, termination, domain randomization, actor and critic network sizes, and evaluation protocol. It uses BeyondMimic’s PPO hyperparameters [3] and 8192 parallel environments per GPU, twice SuperMimic’s. PPO-Sim estimates its policy gradient from sampled simulator rollouts; SuperMimic backpropagates its imagined return through world-model rollouts. SuperMimic reaches a return of 0.82 in evaluation within 1.2 h on one GPU. Within the 18 h budget, PPO-Sim does not reach it. Its final return is 0.60 on one GPU, 0.70 on four, and 0.72 on eight, so going from four to eight GPUs barely helps.

图 4(a) 给出了在 LAFAN1 [28] 上的受控对比。基线 PPO-Sim 是在仿真器中训练的 PPO。PPO-Sim 与 SuperMimic 共享动作、观测、奖励、终止条件、域随机化、行动器与评论器网络尺寸以及评估协议。它使用 BeyondMimic 的 PPO 超参数 [3]，并且每块 GPU 运行 8192 个并行环境，是 SuperMimic 的两倍。PPO-Sim 从采样的仿真器轨迹展开中估计其策略梯度；SuperMimic 则通过世界模型轨迹展开反向传播其想象回报。SuperMimic 在单块 GPU 上于 1.2 小时内就在评估中达到了 0.82 的回报。在 18 小时的预算内，PPO-Sim 未能达到该回报。其最终回报在单块 GPU 上为 0.60，在四块 GPU 上为 0.70，在八块 GPU 上为 0.72，因此从四块 GPU 增加到八块 GPU 几乎没有改善。

图 3(b, c) 显示，对于 SuperMimic 而言，随着数据集时长的增加，达到目标回报所需的 GPU 小时数增长缓慢，而在同一扫描实验中，世界模型的预测误差保持稳定；§4.2 将这两种趋势归因于单一世界模型摊销了跨动作共享的动力学特性。

4.2 Properties of the learned world model

4.2 已学习世界模型的性质

These results rest on three properties of the learned world model \(f_\omega\): its accuracy holds as the motion library grows, and its rollouts are fast and differentiable.

这些结果依赖于已学习世界模型 \(f_\omega\) 的三个性质：随着动作库的增长其精度得以保持，并且其轨迹展开快速且可微。

One model amortizes the motion library. In Figure 3(c), the world model’s 8-step prediction error stays between 0.007 and 0.011 as the dataset grows from 1 to 264 h. Every motion is executed by the same humanoid, so added clips supply more evidence about the shared body and contact dynamics rather than demanding new capacity. This is consistent with the slow growth of the compute needed to reach the target return (§4.1).

单一模型摊销动作库。在图 3(c) 中，随着数据集从 1 小时增加到 264 小时，世界模型的 8 步预测误差保持在 0.007 到 0.011 之间。每个动作均由同一人形机器人执行，因此新增的片段为共享的本体与接触动力学提供了更多证据，而不是需要新的模型容量。这与达到目标回报所需计算量的缓慢增长是一致的（§4.1）。

Imagination accelerates rollouts. During policy optimization, rollout speed sets the update cost. Figure 5(a) shows that the world model runs more than \(10\times\) faster than MuJoCo Warp at the batch sizes used in practice, and up to \(62\times\) at 64k parallel environments.

想象加速轨迹展开。在策略优化期间，轨迹展开速度决定了更新成本。图 5(a) 表明，在实际使用的批大小下，世界模型的运行速度比 MuJoCo Warp 快 \(10\times\) 以上，在 64k 并行环境下最高可达 \(62\times\)。

4.3 A critic enables shorter horizons and push recovery

4.3 评论器实现更短视界与推搡恢复

Figure 4(b, c) sweeps \(H\) with and without the critic. Without the critic, training fails at \(H \le 16\). With it, \(H = 16\) matches the critic-free \(H = 32\), and \(H = 32\) reaches a return of 0.82 fastest. Raising \(H\) from 64 to 128 nearly triples the time to that return and lowers the final return; \(H = 128\) is the longest horizon that fits on an H100. At matched return, the critic still changes behavior. Under 0.5–4 m/s velocity pushes while holding a static T-pose in simulation, the critic policy settles within 0.5–2.4 s. The critic-free policy never settles: it keeps stumbling even at 0.5 m/s and falls at 4 m/s.

图 4(b, c) 扫描了在有和没有评论器情况下的 \(H\)。在没有评论器时，训练在 \(H \le 16\) 时失败。有了评论器，\(H = 16\) 的表现与无评论器的 \(H = 32\) 相当，并且 \(H = 32\) 最快达到 0.82 的回报。将 \(H\) 从 64 增加到 128 使达到该回报的时间几乎增加了两倍，并降低了最终回报；\(H = 128\) 是 H100 上所能容纳的最长视界。在回报相当的情况下，评论器仍然改变了行为表现。在仿真中保持静态 T 姿态并受到 0.5–4 m/s 的速度推搡时，带评论器的策略在 0.5–2.4 秒内稳定下来。而无评论器的策略从未稳定：即使在 0.5 m/s 下它也持续踉跄，并在 4 m/s 时跌倒。

4.4 真机迁移

4.5 Downstream tasks: RL encoder vs. distillation baseline

4.5 下游任务：RL 编码器对比蒸馏基线

SuperMimic trains downstream goal encoders jointly with the motion-tracking interface, sharing one decoder and world model (Appendix B). Our study of downstream interfaces is preliminary. We train one velocity encoder on a velocity-tracking reward through the world model. We compare it with a distillation baseline: a velocity encoder trained on the motion-tracking reward, with commands read from the reference motions. Figure 7 evaluates both on 672 step responses spanning the velocity space (Appendix G). The RL encoder has the lower steady-state error on 650 of them, and the distillation encoder on 15. Only the distillation encoder falls, on seven fast turns with almost no motion-library data.

SuperMimic 将下游目标编码器与动作跟踪接口联合训练，共享同一个解码器和世界模型（附录 B）。我们对下游接口的研究是初步的。我们通过世界模型，在速度跟踪奖励上训练了一个速度编码器。我们将其与蒸馏基线进行对比：后者是在动作跟踪奖励上训练的速度编码器，其指令从参考动作中读取。图 7 在覆盖速度空间的 672 个阶跃响应上对两者进行了评估（附录 G）。RL 编码器在其中 650 个阶跃响应上具有更低的稳态误差，而蒸馏编码器在 15 个上误差更低。只有蒸馏编码器出现了跌倒，发生在几乎没有任何动作库数据的 7 个快速转向指令上。

5 Conclusion

5 结论

SuperMimic removes compute as the bottleneck to scaling humanoid whole-body control. A single learned, differentiable world model captures the dynamics every motion shares, and backpropagation-through-time through it amortizes a whole dataset into one policy. The policy trains on a single GPU, surpasses the return of a policy trained on a 128-GPU cluster, and transfers to a real Unitree G1. Its training cost grows slowly over a controlled dataset-length sweep. The

SuperMimic 消除了算力作为扩展人形机器人全身控制的瓶颈。单个学习得到的、可微的世界模型捕获了所有动作所共享的动力学特性，并且通过该模型进行随时间反向传播，将整个数据集摊销到单一策略中。该策略在单个 GPU 上进行训练，超过了在 128 块 GPU 集群上训练的策略的回报，并能迁移到真实的 Unitree G1 机器人上。在受控的数据集时长扫描实验中，其训练成本增长缓慢。该

6 Limitations and Future Work

6 局限性与未来工作

- Multi-interface training is understudied. We train the velocity encoder concurrently with the motion tracker, but the velocity-tracking reward updates only the velocity encoder and not the shared decoder (Appendix B). Concurrent training is therefore the same as training the velocity encoder after the motion tracker, and might not be necessary. Whether concurrent training helps once the velocity-tracking reward also updates the decoder is untested.

- 多接口训练尚未得到充分研究。我们在训练动作跟踪器的同时并发训练速度编码器，但速度跟踪奖励仅更新速度编码器，而不更新共享解码器（附录 B）。因此，并发训练等同于在动作跟踪器之后训练速度编码器，可能并非必要。一旦速度跟踪奖励也更新解码器，并发训练是否有益尚未经过测试。

- Simulator-only dynamics. The world model learns only from simulator transitions. Real-robot data could refine it, and its differentiability could support system identification.

- 仅基于仿真器的动力学。世界模型仅从仿真器转移数据中学习。真实机器人数据可以对其进行微调，且其可微性可支持系统辨识。

- No terrain or object interaction. SuperMimic trains only on flat ground. Extending it to terrain and object interaction is ongoing work.

- 无地形或物体交互。SuperMimic 仅在平整地面上进行训练。将其扩展至地形和物体交互是正在进行的工作。

A downstream interface is trained by distillation or by RL, depending on its reward. Distillation uses the motion-tracking reward (§3.3), with the command read from the reference motion. The reward gradient then trains that interface to reproduce the reference motion from this command. RL optimizes the interface’s own task reward, such as velocity tracking, directly through the world model. On a downstream interface’s rollouts, the return gradient passes through the shared decoder to that interface’s encoder, with a stop-gradient on the decoder’s weights; the decoder is still trained by the motion-tracking objective. The RL-trained velocity encoder of §4.5 also has a latent-alignment loss toward the motion encoder’s latent on rollouts with reference commands (Appendix G).

下游接口通过蒸馏或 RL 进行训练，具体取决于其奖励形式。蒸馏使用动作跟踪奖励（§3.3），其指令读取自参考动作。随后奖励梯度训练该接口根据此指令复现参考动作。RL 则直接通过世界模型优化该接口自身的任务奖励，例如速度跟踪。在下游接口的轨迹展开中，回报梯度通过共享解码器传递到该接口的编码器，并在解码器的权重上施加截断梯度（stop-gradient）；解码器依然由动作跟踪目标进行训练。§4.5 中通过 RL 训练的速度编码器在带有参考指令的轨迹展开上，还具有针对动作编码器隐变量的隐空间对齐损失（Appendix G）。

G.2 Step test

G.2 阶跃测试

Protocol. From standing, the policy receives a fixed command for 5 s; each command is tested in four trials. The relative steady-state error is the norm of the mean velocity error over the last 2 s, divided by the norm of the command, with the root velocity measured in the heading frame. The per-command value is the median over the trials that did not fall. The 672 step responses form a \(15\times15\) grid on each of three planes, spanning the uniform command range above, with the standing command left out. In Figure 7, errors above 0.9 are drawn at 0.9, and a policy that fell in all four trials is drawn at the cap.

测试方案。从站立状态开始，策略接收一个持续 5 s 的固定指令；每个指令在四次试验中进行测试。相对稳态误差是最后 2 s 内平均速度误差的范数除以指令的范数，其中根部速度在航向坐标系下测量。每个指令的值是未摔倒试验的中位数。672 个阶跃响应在三个平面中的每一个上形成 \(15\times15\) 的网格，覆盖上述均匀指令范围，但不包含站立指令。在图 7 中，大于 0.9 的误差均绘制在 0.9 处，在所有四次试验中均摔倒的策略则绘制在上限截断处。

Results. The RL encoder has the lower error on 650 of the 672 step responses, and the distillation encoder on 15. Their median relative errors are 0.16 and 0.49. Distillation falls in every trial on seven commands, all combining translation with a turn of at least \(2.8\,\mathrm{rad/s}\), where the motion library has almost no data. 结果。强化学习编码器在 672 个阶跃响应中的 650 个上具有更低的误差，而蒸馏编码器仅在 15 个上误差更低。它们的中位数相对误差分别为 0.16 和 0.49。蒸馏策略在 7 个指令的所有试验中均发生摔倒，这些指令均结合了平移与至少 \(2.8\,\mathrm{rad/s}\) 的转向，而动作库在这些区域几乎没有数据。每个策略均使用单一训练随机种子。
