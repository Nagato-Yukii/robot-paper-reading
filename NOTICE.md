# NOTICE

This repository contains **bilingual reading notes** for robotics papers.

- The English text, figures and tables come from the original papers, whose
  copyright belongs to their authors. They are reproduced here in excerpted
  form (figures and tables re-cropped from the publicly available arXiv PDFs)
  for the purpose of study, translation and commentary.
- The Chinese translations, the site templates and all code in `pipeline/`
  are original work of this repository and are released under the MIT license.

Included papers:

1. *Scaling Online Exploration for Robot Agents* — Runhan Huang et al.
2. *Bundled Contact Gradients: Stabilizing Differentiable Simulation for
   Deployable Dynamic Tasks* — Dyuman Aditya et al. (arXiv:2609.30951)

If you are an author and would like your paper, its figures, or its PDF
removed from this repository, please open an issue and it will be removed
promptly.

Translation disclaimer: the translations are produced with AI assistance and
reviewed for technical terminology; they are intended as reading aids, not as
authoritative versions. Where a wording differs, the original paper governs.
